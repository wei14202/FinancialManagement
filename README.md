# Financial Management Toolkit

A Python-based application designed to help individuals with EPF/CPF account to manage their finances effectively by accounting for CPF contributions, AWS (13th-month bonus), and long-term savings projections.

## Features

* **Salary & EPF/CPF Calculator**: Calculates monthly take-home pay based on current CPF contribution rates and the CPF Ordinary Wage (OW) ceiling.
* **Bonus Integration**: Includes AWS (13th-month salary) support to provide a more accurate annual income assessment.
* **Age-Specific Calculations**: Adjusts CPF contribution rates dynamically for different age groups up to 55.
* **Savings Projection**: Forecasts your total savings over a period of $n$ years using compound interest formulas, accounting for different account interest rates (CPF/EPF).
* **Expense Tracker**: A built-in calculator to balance your monthly expenditures based on your net income.

### Prerequisites

* Python 3.x installed on your  system.
* [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io/) for the user interface.

## Usage
The application is designed to be interactive. Simply input your monthly gross salary, age, and expected annual expenses. The dashboard will automatically calculate your:
*   Net Monthly Take-home Pay.
*   Annual CPF contribution breakdown.
*   Projected savings growth over time.
